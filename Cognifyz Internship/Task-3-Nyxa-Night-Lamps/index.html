Shipping & returns

Each lamp is hand-finished to order, so please build the processing time into your timeline.

Processing
3–5 business days before your order leaves the workshop.
Delivery
4–7 business days domestic, 10–16 business days international.
Packaging
Glass shade and base are boxed separately inside one outer carton, cushioned in molded pulp.
Returns
30 days from delivery, unused and in original packaging. Custom finishes are final sale.
Warranty
2 years on the LED driver and circuit; lifetime support on the glass and ash base.
